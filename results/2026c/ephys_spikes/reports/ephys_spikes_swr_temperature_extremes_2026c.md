## Definitions

- **Hottest window** of biological date D: +-1 h around the 5-min weather sample with the highest outdoor temperature between sunrise and sunset of D. **Coldest window**: +-1 h around the lowest between sunset D and sunrise D+1.
- **Window rate** $R = \sum n_k / \sum N_k$ over the 30-min bins whose centre lies in the window (kept NREM SWR events per NREM minute); >= 10 NREM minutes required.
- **Paired difference** $\Delta = R_{cold} - R_{hot}$ per animal x date x block; pooled mean with SEs clustered by date (CR1, $t(G-1)$); animal-level = the per-animal mean differences, one-sample t.
