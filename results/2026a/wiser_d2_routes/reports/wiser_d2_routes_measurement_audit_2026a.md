## Per-finding classification — newly audited pieces

**(a) bout_length — "~4 s / ~100 in capacity" → likely MEASUREMENT ARTIFACT (segmentation-defined).** The tracker's "SEGMENTATION ARTIFACT" flag holds as a measurement statement. The scale moves ~1:1 with the analyst-imposed 3 s min-bout filter: displacement median = 21.6 + **25.0·min_bout** (slope = the median speed 25 in/s), duration median = 0.89 + **0.95·min_bout**. "100 in" = 3 s × 25 in/s + offset; "~4 s" = the conditional median of a **0.54 s** (un-truncated) distribution cut at 3 s. The termination hazard is non-monotone lognormal with its only breakpoint at ~1.3 s = the 1 s speed-smoothing window (a measurement floor), so a genuine "characteristic run duration" is **falsified**. The pattern is universal across animals/nights/cohort splits — invariance is the artifact signature, not biology. Measurement-supported residual: rats move in ~straight, ~constant-speed ballistic primitives — but the primitive *timescale* is sub-second at the ~4 Hz sampling / ~2-sample floor (upper-bounded, not positively measured), and even "25 in/s" is a frame-internal ratio. **Load-bearing cross-report flag:** the report's own load-bearing caveat is correct, but its verdict-D phrase "punctuated by reorientation pauses" (A7: 64° across a pause vs 16° within) is precisely what decision_boundary_validation later falsifies — read that sub-clause as SUPERSEDED.

**(b) bout_segmentation_validation (verdict D) → measurement-sound FALSIFICATION (with two caveats).** The verdict-D reasoning (scale is segmentation-defined; A falsified; true structure = ballistic primitives that are fragments of longer multi-stop episodes) is correct and rigorous (engine validated against the pipeline first; 99% of bouts merge into paddock-scale episodes at a 5 s pause-bridge — so "never crosses the paddock" was itself an artifact). Caveats: (i) the "reorientation pauses" sub-result (A7) is **not** measurement-supported — falsified by decision_boundary_validation (below); the report's own top-note already retracted "near-memoryless" and "trip→merged episode," and this extends that correction. (ii) Method-provenance: scipy absent → hazard/MLE done in numpy with an approximated truncated-gamma normalization; no commit stamped in the report header.

**(c) decision_boundary_validation — "reorientation" reading → likely MEASUREMENT ARTIFACT (jitter); jitter-null logic CONFIRMED.** The falsification is measurement-sound and, importantly, uses two independent controls that agree: (1) a jitter-only null — a *straight* simulated path (real speed profile incl. sub-threshold pauses, per-axis σ=5 in ≈ 7 in radial floor, same smoothing) — reproduces a matched pause-vs-continuous turn of **+20.4°**, *larger* than the real **+17.9°**; (2) a simulation-independent restriction to well-resolved headings (both flanks ≥30 in ≫ floor) makes the effect vanish and **reverse to −3.1°**. The changepoint detector is 30–77% false-positive with 4–24% sensitivity; the pause "predictability" advantage is a speed confound (near-stationary → trivially "stay-put" predictable). Root cause is structural: pauses are intrinsically low-displacement, so their headings are jitter-limited by construction — WISER (~7 in, ~4.4 Hz) is the wrong instrument. Discipline is correct: Stages 2/3 (legs, leg-vocabulary, policy) were **not built** per the staging gate. The one "weakly supported" residual (post-pause heading better predicted by location, 1.15→0.83 rad) is correctly hedged as geometry/route-structure (consistent with endpoint dominance), not a proven decision. **This is the direction's cleanest measurement result and a sibling-handoff trigger** (needs CV pose/keypoints).

**(d) following_incidents (Phase B2, 2046 episodes) → MIXED / temporal-order candidate; count is floor-anchored, "leader" is confounded.** "Leader/follower = temporal order, not geometry" is stated. Measurement notes: (i) median episode duration **3 s = the min_bout_s floor** → "frequent but brief" is partly definitional (the duration is floor-pinned; the *count* is the interpretable statistic, and even it depends on merge/lag params).
