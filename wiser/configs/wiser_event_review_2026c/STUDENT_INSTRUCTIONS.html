Rat Video Review

Instructions for reviewers. Read once before you start (about 10 minutes). The review itself takes about 2–3 hours for 80 short events; you can split it over several sessions.

What you are doing. Rats wearing a small radio tag lived in an outdoor enclosure. A radio tracking system ("the tracker") recorded where each rat was. Sometimes the tracker says a rat moved or turned when its head sensor says it did not. You will watch short video clips of those moments and record what the rat really did. Your answers are used as the ground truth for checking the tracker, so please judge only what you can see.

1. Before you start

  • Use a desktop or laptop computer with Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (Safari and phones are not supported). No internet connection or installation is needed.
  • Keep the folder wiser_event_review_2026c_student exactly as it is: index.html must stay next to the folders clips, clips_reveal and panels. You may work directly from this drive, or copy the whole folder (about 11 GB) to your own disk first — playback is smoother from a local disk.
  • The clips are night infrared video: black and white, sometimes grainy. That is normal.
  • Do not share or upload the videos.

2. Start

  1. Double-click index.html. If it opens in another browser, right-click it → Open with → Chrome or Edge.
  2. Type your initials in the Reviewer box at the top. Use the same initials every session.
  3. The list on the left shows 80 events, W01 … W80, in random order. Work through them in any order; n / p go to the next / previous event. Help (?) at the top repeats the key information.

3. For each event

Step A — find the rat. Each clip starts 10 s before the event and ends 10 s after it; a red EVENT box in the banner and a red stretch of the time bar mark the event. Usually two camera views are shown. The banner names the rat and its marks. At night the video is black and white, so use the IR pattern on the rat's back (table below), not the colour. A dashed white circle labelled "start" shows where the tracker placed the rat just before the event — use it to find the right rat. Some clips have no circle ("no projection — use the IR mark"); then find the rat by its pattern.

Step B — answer the first question (keys 1 2 3). There are two kinds of event; the page shows which.

EventQuestionAnswers
Place (I1)Did this rat actually change place during the EVENT? 1 moved — the whole body went to a new spot at least about one body length (≈ 30 cm / 1 ft) away.
2 stayed — it stayed on the same spot: turning round, grooming, sniffing, rearing, shuffling inside a huddle all count as stayed.
3 cannot tell.
Turn (I2)Did the rat's body turn sharply during the EVENT? 1 turned sharply — the body changed direction by about 90° or more.
2 ran straight — straight or only a gentle curve; a head turn alone does not count.
3 cannot tell.

Choose cannot tell whenever you are not sure — that is a useful answer, not a failure. Then press the reason: 4 rat not visible, 5 not sure it is the named rat, 6 other (write it in the note). Add a short note whenever something is unusual.

Step C — save (Enter). Your first saved answer is locked as your blind answer. The clip then changes:

  • a solid yellow circle = where the tracker placed the rat, moving over time;
  • a red dot = the tracker's raw reading;
  • a side panel with the tracker and sensor numbers.

Step D — answer the second question (keys 7 8 9): Does the yellow circle stay on the rat? 7 on the rat (it follows the rat) · 8 drifts off the rat (it wanders away from where the rat really is) · 9 cannot tell. If the clip has no circle, this question is skipped automatically.
If, at the very start of the event, the dashed "start" circle was clearly not on the rat, press m ("start circle off").

Step E — next event (Enter again). Please do not change your first answer after seeing the circles unless you pressed a wrong key; any change is recorded separately.

5. Saving and returning your answers

Answers are saved automatically, but only inside this browser on this computer. At the end of every session:

  1. Click Export JSON and then Export CSV. The browser saves two files named like wiser_event_review_2026c_<your initials>_<date>.json / .csv, usually into your Downloads folder.
  2. Move both files into the exports folder of this drive.

When you have finished all 80 events, export once more and return the drive (or send the two newest files) to the person who gave it to you.

Changing computer or browser? Your answers do not move with you: on the new computer click Import and choose your latest .json export, then continue. Do not clear the browser's site data while you are working.

6. If something does not work

  • Page is empty or videos do not play: use Chrome or Edge, and check that index.html is still next to the clips and clips_reveal folders.
  • Video stutters: copy the whole folder to your computer's disk and open it from there, or pause and step with the arrow keys.
  • A clip shows "NO VIDEO" for a moment: a short gap in the original recording; answer from the rest of the clip.
  • Answers disappeared: you are in another browser, profile or private window — go back, or use Import.

Questions: ask the person who gave you this drive. Thank you!
