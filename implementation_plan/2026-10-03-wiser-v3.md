# V3 — one B2 filter with a firm IMU zero-velocity constraint and locomotion-boosted process noise (cohort 2026c)

**Status:** PLANNED 2026-10-03, written before any V3 number was computed.
**Approval:** the user approved this step on 2026-10-03 ("做") after steps A–C. It is the first WISER-smoother test whose
motion criterion is referenced to a smoother-independent truth (clean-WISER speed from step B) instead of to B2.
Operational details are marked **[op]**.
**Direction:** `wiser_baseline`. **Driver:** new `wiser/scripts/analyze_wiser_v3.py` (imports, never edits,
`analyze_wiser_v1b.py`, `analyze_imu_speed_proxy.py`, `analyze_wiser_default_smoother.py`, `analyze_wiser_failure_audit.py`,

## Why (established facts, not re-derived)

- **Step A** (`change_log/2026-10-03-wiser-v1b-zupt.md`): V1b (B2 + Huber ZUPT σ 1 in/s in eroded still runs, release
  after > 5 s of ≥ 12-in drift) passed its identity claim and is the default for implanted animals. Its ZUPT is soft
  (still fake path 38.9 | 50.8 → 12.8 | 20.0 in/min; σ 0.25 without guards gave 3.3 | 3.6), and 108 of its 155 releases
  fell inside certified stillness (the release mostly follows WISER drift). Its E1b "failures" of the variants came from
  IMU-still seconds inside the WISER-fast subset (a flaw of that subset, not motion).
- **Step B** (`change_log/2026-10-03-imu-speed-proxy.md`): the IMU proxy is not valid, but clean-WISER speed is a usable
  smoother-independent reference. At the 3-s p95 of the > 15 in/s band: B2 = V1b −8.0 %, V2 −4.3 %, V2b −1.4 %, B2′/V1
  −18.5 %, B1 −0.7 %; 3-s p95 over all clean seconds: B2 −5.2 %, V2b −2.5 %. NIS under B2 ≈ 2.4–2.6 still but 8–10
  locomoting: the constant-velocity q (3 in²/s³) is too stiff in runs.
- **Step C**: cross-tag common mode is not material.

So the two levers are known and separable: a firm ZUPT where the head is still, and more process noise only where the IMU
says locomotion. V2b already used q × 10 in locomotion but also lowered q in "active" (× 0.3) and "still" (× 0.01) and was
spliced to B2; V3 changes **only** the locomotion q and adds the ZUPT, in one filter.

## Candidate

**V3 (primary).** The pilot's B2 (q = 3 in²/s³, per-fix noise from `anchors_used`, χ² gate + 2 Huber IRLS passes,
k_H = 2.5, forward KF + RTS), with:
1. **Process noise by IMU state:** q × **10** on a step whose midpoint second is IMU-QC-ok and `locomoting` (pilot rule);
   × 1 everywhere else (still, active, QC-failed, margins). The × 10 is the pilot's tuned m_loco, used unchanged.
2. **ZUPT:** pseudo-measurements $0=v_x$, $0=v_y$, **σ = 0.25 in/s**, Huber-weighted (k_H = 2.5, inside the IRLS passes,
   as in V1b), at fixes inside runs of IMU-QC-ok `still` seconds of length ≥ 3 s, **eroded by 1 s** at each end. **No
   release.**
3. Where the IMU QC fails: no ZUPT and q × 1 → B2 dynamics in the same filter; no splice.

**Sensitivities (reported, never the decision):** V3 with σ_ZUPT 0.5 and 1.0; V3 with loco multiplier × 3 and × 30; V3
without the ZUPT ("loco-only"). Context tracks (scored, not eligible here): B2, V1b, V2b.

## Pre-registered acceptance

- **T1 — fast motion followed (truth-referenced).** On step B's clean seconds (all six nights: 09-03, 09-05 … 09-09; the
  same clean filter and 3-s speed estimator, the track's speed computed from the same medians of the track as the
  reference uses of the raw fixes), the relative difference to clean-WISER speed is **within ± 3 %** for both (a) the 3-s
  p95 over all clean seconds and (b) the 3-s p95 of the > 15 in/s band (point estimates; paired 10-min block-bootstrap CIs
  reported). Also reported: the 5–15 in/s band p50, the 1-s scale, and per animal.
- **T2 — no jumps.** 0 jumps (> 30 in within ≤ 0.35 s) in the primary certified still segments (calm and rain) and over
  the analysis mask of every audit period.
- **T3 — transitions not later than B2.** S5 events (default-smoother definition, all audit periods): median per-event
  Δ = lag(V3) − lag(B2) **≤ + 0.5 s** for onsets and for offsets, calm and rain (one-sided: earlier is allowed; point
  estimates; CIs and the share of shifted events reported).

**Decision.** V3 passes T1–T3 → **V3 replaces V1b as the default WISER track for the implanted animals** (IMU-QC-ok
stretches; B2 elsewhere by construction); **B2 stays the universal baseline**. V3 fails any → V1b stays the default; the
report names the failed criterion. No sensitivity variant is promoted here (a passing variant becomes a proposal to the
user).

## Caveats known in advance

T1's reference exists only on clean open-field seconds (≥ 8 anchors, no jumps) — speed under poor anchors and in the
houses stays unreferenced. The > 15 in/s band is chosen on the noisy reference (selection regression, small at 3 s). The
locomotion class (TPR 0.75 / FPR 0.15) was fitted against WISER speed. × 10 was tuned on 09-08/09, one of the nights T1
uses. Still metrics are circular; ≥ 95 % of certified stillness is in the houses.
